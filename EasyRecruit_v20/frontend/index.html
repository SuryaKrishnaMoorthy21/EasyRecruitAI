🎯 How Scoring Works
Keywords — vocabulary overlap with the job description, or general resume coherence if you don't paste one.
Skills — how many of the role's actual required skills were found in your resume text.
Structure — real section headers, bullet points, dates, and contact info, not just running prose.
A document with no detectable name, skills, or resume sections gets flagged instead of scored — garbage in shouldn't mean a confident score out.
💡 Quick Tips
  • Use bullet points under each role instead of paragraphs
  • Add a dedicated Skills section — skills only mentioned in passing score lower
  • Quantify achievements where you can, e.g. "cut load time by 30%"
  • Paste the job description above — matching gets far more accurate with one
